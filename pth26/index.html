Abstract

Loops are a key primitive for autonomous AI agents, and the now widely used "Agent Mode".

This session dives into the journey from the core Reason + Act loop to loop engineering — the shift from prompting agents to designing the systems that prompt them.

And while the loops run — and the lights go out on fully automated Dark Software Factories — what should we focus on?

Resources & links

  • ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models (Yao et al.) ↗The paper behind the Reason + Act loop.
  • Inner Monologue: Embodied Reasoning through Planning with Language Models (Huang et al.) ↗Closing the loop: feeding environment feedback back into the model.
  • The Law of Leaky Abstractions (Joel Spolsky) ↗Why every abstraction, agents included, eventually leaks.
  • The Five Levels: from Spicy Autocomplete to the Dark Factory (Dan Shapiro) ↗A ladder of AI coding autonomy, up to the lights-out software factory.
  • Loop Engineering (Addy Osmani) ↗From writing prompts to designing the loops that prompt coding agents.
  • The Factory Model: How Coding Agents Changed Software Engineering (Addy Osmani) ↗Building the factory that builds your software.
